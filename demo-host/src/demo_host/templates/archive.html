File a paper document

A document that was signed in ink. Upload the scan and say what it is; the signing service stores it write-once, seals it with a timestamp, and it lands in the chart with the same verification as anything signed electronically.

What the seal proves: that the scan has not changed since you filed it, and that you attested to it. What it does not prove: that the ink signature is genuine. That rests on the paper original and on you. The cover page inside the sealed PDF says exactly this.

No scanner handy? Download the sample scan, a two-page image-only PDF, and file that.
