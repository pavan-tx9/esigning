People

Everybody who signs here, and the one thing this records system may do about a saved signature.

A person can save the signature they adopt so their next session offers it again. Only they can create one, from inside their own signing session; there is no call for a host to upload a signature for somebody, so staff cannot make a doctor's signature. Either side can remove one. The service does not say whether a person has one saved, so this page cannot either.
